# My Savings – Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** November 18, 2026

**Developer:** My Savings  
**Support Email:** techvanta.global@gmail.com

## 1. Introduction

My Savings is a personal savings management application designed to help users record and track their savings in AED and BDT currencies.

We respect your privacy. My Savings is designed to work offline and store your financial records locally on your device.

## 2. Information We Store

My Savings may store the following information locally on your device:

- Savings deposits and withdrawals
- Savings categories
- Transaction dates, amounts and notes
- App PIN and security preferences
- App settings

The app does not require users to create an account.

## 3. Data Collection and Sharing

The current app version does not include an Internet permission or an integrated analytics or advertising service.

We do not collect, transmit, sell or share your financial records through the app.

## 4. Local Storage and Backup

Your savings records are stored locally on your device.

You can manually export a JSON backup using the app's Backup feature. You choose where to save that file.

Backup files saved outside the app are subject to the security and privacy of the location where you store them. We do not receive these files.

## 5. Biometric Authentication

The app may use Android's biometric authentication feature, such as fingerprint authentication, if enabled by the user.

Biometric verification is handled by the Android operating system. My Savings does not receive or store your fingerprint or biometric information.

## 6. Data Deletion

You can delete individual transactions or categories using the app's available features.

Uninstalling the app may remove its locally stored data. Any JSON backup files saved separately must be deleted separately by the user.

## 7. Children's Privacy

My Savings is a general personal savings management app and is not specifically designed for children.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when app features or privacy practices change. The latest version should be made available through the official Privacy Policy page.
